03 — About

about.

Isabelle Lee is a multidisciplinary visual artist and storyteller working in cinematography, photography, filmmaking, mixed media, and graphic design. Based between New York and Los Angeles, her practice is rooted in experimentation, combining digital and analog processes to explore texture, emotion, and the complexity of human experience. Her focus is telling the stories of people and communities that mainstream media tends to overlook, flatten, and stereotype. She approaches her subjects with curiosity and care.

Her work spans narrative and editorial film, darkroom and digital photography, mixed-media pieces, and visual design. She's currently pursuing a B.A. in Cinema & Media Studies at USC's School of Cinematic Arts. More of her photography, design work, and other ongoing projects are on her Instagram, linked below.
